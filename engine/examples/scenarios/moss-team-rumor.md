MOSS is a fictional token made up for this simulation. It launched two weeks ago with a 1% swap fee and about 8 ETH against 100 million MOSS in its pool, and it has a quiet crowd of holders. The team wallet holds 12% of the supply and has not moved it since launch.

This morning an anonymous account posted a screenshot and claimed the team is "about to sell everything". The screenshot shows a balance with no address, so nobody can check it. The team has not answered. Holders are split between people who want proof first and people who would rather leave now and ask questions later.

The pool is thin: a single 0.4 ETH swap moves the price by roughly 10%, so a few nervous wallets can make the chart look like confirmation.

## Events

- round 3: A second anonymous account repeats the rumor and says it has "inside info". It shows nothing new.
- round 5: On-chain data shows the team wallet moved 3% of the supply to a new wallet. It is not an exchange address, and nothing has been sold.
- round 8: The team posts that the transfer funds a six-month vesting contract, and publishes the contract address.
