GLINT is a fictional meme token made up for this simulation. In this scenario it has just launched on Pons with a 5% creator tax on every trade. The simulated pool models the tax as a 5% swap fee on every buy and sell and leaves other launchpad fees out, so a quick round trip costs close to 10% before price impact.

The launch post promises "community first" and a roadmap "soon". There is no website yet, only a launch page at https://pons.example/glint?ref=launch. The creator wallet holds 15% of the supply and has not said what the tax revenue is for.

Liquidity is thin: about 5 ETH against 100 million GLINT, so a single 0.25 ETH swap moves the price by roughly 10%. Early chatter is split between wallets excited about a fast start and wallets asking why the tax is so high and who receives it.

## Events

- round 3: A wallet that entered in the first minute posts that it is up 3x and has taken most of its position off the table.
- round 5: Rumor: the creator wallet moved 20% of its GLINT to an exchange deposit address. Nothing on-chain confirms a sale yet.
- round 8: The creator posts that the tax drops to 1% after the first week and that tax revenue funds a liquidity lock.
