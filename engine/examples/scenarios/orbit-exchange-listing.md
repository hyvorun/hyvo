ORBIT is a fictional token made up for this simulation. For a month it has traded in one pool with a 0.3% swap fee and about 12 ETH of liquidity. Northgate, a small fictional exchange, has just announced that it will list ORBIT, and nobody on the team mentioned it beforehand.

Some wallets read the listing as the start of real attention. Others point out that small exchanges list many tokens, that Northgate's volume is hard to verify, and that the team has not said whether it paid a listing fee.

A single 0.6 ETH swap moves the pool price by roughly 10%. The pool is the only market open so far.

## Events

- round 3: Northgate opens deposits. A large wallet moves 4% of the supply to its deposit address.
- round 5: Trading opens on Northgate. Its first reported price is 6% above the pool, on thin volume.
- round 8: Northgate pauses withdrawals of ORBIT for "scheduled maintenance" and gives no end time.
